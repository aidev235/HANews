## 2. Counterexamples to Schiffer's Conjecture

**Authors:** Gonzalo Cao-Labora, Jaume de Dios Pont
**Source/event:** [arXiv:2608.05114v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.05114v1), new preprint submitted August 5, 2026.
**Topics:** Schiffer conjecture, Pompeiu problem, Laplace eigenfunctions, bifurcation theory, planar domains.

**What happened.** The authors construct infinitely many smooth planar domains that are not disks but admit Neumann eigenfunctions constant on the boundary. This disproves Schiffer's conjecture in two dimensions and, through its close relation to the Pompeiu problem, the corresponding planar rigidity claim. Their method relaxes the order of rotational symmetry to a real parameter and uses uniform control of bifurcation branches to recover integer symmetry orders.

**Assessment and caveats.** The construction turns a longstanding rigidity question into a flexible bifurcation phenomenon and supplies an infinite family rather than a single exceptional domain. A distinct [computer-assisted construction](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.01579v1), posted just outside the local reporting window, supports the broad failure through a different method but does not independently validate this proof. The selected source is a first-version, unrefereed preprint whose bifurcation and regularity arguments still require specialist checking.

## 3. The Minimum Cardinality of a Dependent Finite Gabor System Is Four

**Authors:** Xinan Dai, Wenhao Deng, Yingdong Shi, Tailin Wu, Yuchen Yang
**Source/event:** [arXiv:2608.08190v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.08190v1), new preprint submitted August 8, 2026.
**Topics:** finite Gabor systems, HRT conjecture, Zak bundles, dominated splittings, interval arithmetic.

**What happened.** Following the twelve-shift HRT counterexample, the authors construct a dependent system of only four time-frequency shifts of a Schwartz function. Since every system of at most three shifts of a nonzero $L^2(\mathbb{R})$ function is known to be independent, the paper identifies four as the exact minimum cardinality.

**Assessment and caveats.** The result sharpens the week's main HRT development from existence to the smallest possible configuration. Its proof combines a rank-two Zak-bundle construction, a dominated contracting line, a winding argument, and a finite outward-rounded interval certificate. A contemporaneous four-point construction overlaps semantically, while the selected manuscript's sharp minimality and certificate remain unrefereed and need independent review.

## 4. The trace-free Beurling--Ahlfors transform and the Bourgain--Brezis problem for Hodge systems

**Author:** Diogo Arsénio
**Source/event:** [arXiv:2608.04237v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.04237v1), new preprint submitted August 4, 2026.
**Topics:** Bourgain–Brezis estimates, Hodge systems, Beurling–Ahlfors transforms, endpoint cancellation, critical function spaces.

**What happened.** Arsénio introduces a trace-free normalization of the generalized Beurling–Ahlfors transform whose symbol has reflection-odd scalar components. The resulting endpoint cancellation completes the Hilbertian Bourgain–Brezis conjecture for every dimension and form degree. Multilinear reflection estimates also yield critical Triebel–Lizorkin and Besov results and Sobolev estimates for a sequence of exponents approaching one.

**Assessment and caveats.** The work proposes a reusable operator-theoretic mechanism for endpoint Hodge estimates, connecting singular-integral symmetry to Bourgain–Brezis compensation. It does not claim the full Sobolev conjecture for every exponent near one, and most associated bounded selections are shown to be necessarily nonlinear. The broad all-dimension conclusions rest on an unrefereed first version.

**Authors:** Andrei Caragea, Mihail N. Kolountzakis, Götz Pfander
**Source/event:** [arXiv:2608.06679v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.06679v1), new preprint submitted August 6, 2026; a v2 update followed outside the window.
**Topics:** Gabor frames, Schwartz windows, Feichtinger algebra, lattice tilings and packings, Balian–Low theorems.

**What happened.** The authors characterize exactly which general time-frequency lattices admit Schwartz-class Gabor-frame windows and which admit windows in the Feichtinger algebra. **Assessment and caveats.** The result connects a basic existence problem in Gabor analysis to lattice geometry and produces sharp Balian–Low consequences. The characterization concerns lattice Gabor systems and should not be read as covering arbitrary irregular time-frequency sets. This report assesses the in-window v1; the manuscript remains unrefereed.
